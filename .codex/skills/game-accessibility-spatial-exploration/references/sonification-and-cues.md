# Sonification and Cues

Use this file when speech alone is too slow or too dense for spatial play.

## What Cues Are Good For

- mode changes
- successful movement
- blocked movement
- target acquired
- target lost
- hazard proximity
- directional guidance
- scanner category changes

## Rules

- Keep cues consistent across the whole mod.
- Use pitch, repetition rate, pan, and timbre deliberately.
- Do not make speech and cues compete for the same event unless both are needed.
- Use speech for identity and detail; use cues for timing, direction, and urgency.

## Common Layout

- short confirmation cue for valid movement
- distinct error cue for blocked movement
- directional or panned cue for nearby targets
- urgency cue for danger, combat, or low resources
- overlay-specific cue family when changing spatial modes
